About This Gallery

This video gallery presents six short videos of some of Ghana's most beautiful and historic tourist sites. I chose this theme to match my image gallery and to show that Ghana is a great destination for history, nature and culture.

Viewers should expect to see castles, waterfalls, national parks and cultural landmarks in motion. Each video has a title and a short description explaining what it shows.
