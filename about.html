About This Website

Our Purpose

The Ghana Tourist Sites Gallery is a web project created to help people around the world discover the beauty, history and culture of Ghana. The website brings together photographs and videos of ten iconic tourist sites in Ghana, from the coastal castles of Cape Coast and Elmina to the waterfalls, national parks and cultural landmarks of the country.

The purpose of this website is to make travel information simple and enjoyable. Visitors can browse images of famous sites, watch short videos of the same locations, read about what each site means, and learn about the recommended order to visit them.

About the Content

The image gallery showcases historic forts, waterfalls, national parks and cultural heritage sites. The video gallery presents short clips of the same places so visitors can see Ghana in motion. Each image and video is linked to a description and an information table that explains its history and significance.
